What is AI video ad production?

AI video ads are advertising videos created with the help of generative AI for scripts, scenes, voiceovers or edits. They allow brands to produce many variations quickly and test which creative performs best.

Creative is usually the biggest lever in paid media, and ads wear out fast. The more quality concepts you can test, the better your chance of finding a winner. AI makes each new version cheaper.
