What is Keyword Research?

Keyword research is the process of finding the words and questions people use when searching for a product, service or topic. It analyzes search volume, difficulty, intent and business value to decide which pages to create or improve.

What is keyword research?

Keyword research finds the terms and questions people search for, then evaluates volume, difficulty, intent and business value.

How do you find high-intent keywords?

Look for words that signal buying, such as pricing, best, vs and service terms. Then validate them against your sales conversations.

What are long-tail keywords?

They are longer, more specific searches. They often have lower volume but clearer intent and less competition.

How many keywords should one page target?

One primary topic plus closely related variations that share the same intent.

What is a topic cluster?

It's a group of related pages linked around a main pillar page, showing depth on a subject.

Guide

The complete guide to Keyword Research

Plain answers on the topics buyers search for most.

keyword research serviceskeyword difficultysearch intent mappingtopic clustersAI prompt research

What keyword research services deliver

Keyword research services show what your buyers search for, how hard each topic is to win and what it could be worth. The result should be a prioritized plan rather than a giant spreadsheet. Every keyword group should map to a page and a purpose.

How to read keyword difficulty

Keyword difficulty scores are estimates, and they differ between tools. Look at who actually ranks. If the results are full of major brands with strong pages, plan for a longer effort, or start with a narrower topic you can win sooner.

Search intent mapping

Intent mapping sorts keywords by what the searcher wants: to learn, compare or buy. Then each group gets the right page type. This stops several pages competing for the same search and shows which stages of the journey you are missing.

Building topic clusters

A topic cluster links a broad pillar page to detailed supporting articles. It shows depth on a subject and gives readers a clear route through it. Choose clusters that lead naturally to what you sell, not just topics with high volume.

Adding AI prompts to keyword research

Modern research should include the longer questions people ask AI assistants. Mine sales calls, forums and People Also Ask. These prompts often show the detail and comparisons buyers care about, and they can guide both pages and FAQs.
