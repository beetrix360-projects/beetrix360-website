What is Google Ads management?

Google Ads management is the ongoing work of planning, building and optimizing advertising campaigns on Google. It includes keyword and audience targeting, ad copy, bidding, landing pages, conversion tracking and reporting across Search, Shopping, Display and YouTube.

Google's automation is only as smart as the signals you give it. If your account optimizes for form fills, it will find cheap form fills. We import qualified leads and closed deals, so bidding learns what a valuable customer looks like.

Then we tighten everything around that signal: search term reviews, negative keywords, match type strategy, ad relevance and landing page experience. You get fewer wasted clicks and a clear view of what each campaign returns.

Search, Performance Max and Shopping

Search captures clear intent. Performance Max and Shopping add reach across Google's inventory. We structure them so they support each other and don't simply claim your brand searches.

Quality Score and landing pages

Relevant ads and useful landing pages can lower your costs. We work on message match and page speed, then test offers that convert.

What does Google Ads management include?

It includes keyword research, campaign setup, ad copy, bidding, negative keywords, conversion tracking, landing page advice and reporting.

How much should I spend on Google Ads?

Base your budget on cost per click, conversion rate and target cost per sale. The calculator on this page shows how those numbers connect.

Is Performance Max better than Search?

They do different jobs. Search gives more control over intent, while Performance Max reaches across Google's channels. Many accounts use both.

Why are my Google Ads not converting?

Common causes are broad targeting, weak offers, slow landing pages or broken tracking. An audit quickly shows which one applies.

What is a good Quality Score?

Scores of seven or higher are generally healthy, but the score is only a diagnostic. Profit and cost per sale matter more.

Can you take over our existing account?

Yes. You keep ownership of the account and its history. We audit it first, then improve it in stages.

How soon can we expect results from Google Ads?

It depends on your starting point and market. We share a realistic timeline after the audit, with milestones you can track from the first month.

Who is Google Ads best for?

It suits businesses that want a clear return from their marketing and growth work. The audit shows whether it is the right next step for you, or whether something else should come first.

What do you need from us to start Google Ads?

A short kickoff call, access to the relevant accounts and your main goals. We send a simple checklist after the audit so nothing is missed.

How do you measure results from Google Ads?

We agree on the numbers that matter before work starts, such as leads, pipeline or revenue. You get plain-language reports that show what changed and what comes next.

Can Google Ads work alongside our in-house team?

Yes. We can lead the work, support your team, or hand over what we build with documentation and training.

Do you guarantee results from Google Ads?

No honest partner can promise exact outcomes, because results depend on your market and many outside factors. We do commit to clear goals, transparent reporting and steady work on the highest-value priorities.
